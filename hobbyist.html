Digital inspections

Log it your way.

Every beekeeper has their own style, and PollenPal adapts to it. A customizable form covers frames of bees and brood, honey stores, mite counts, queen sightings, temperament, and feeding. Tap through it at the hive and you are done. Spotty signal in the bee yard? Every tap is saved as you go.

Reminders and next visits

Your bees don't follow a calendar. Neither should your app.

Spring in Southern California is not spring in Minnesota. Set reminders that fit your climate: mite tests, feeding, requeening, anything you like, repeating every year if you want. Jot next-visit notes while you are still at the box, and PollenPal flags the hive that has gone too long without a look.

Your apiary

Your apiary. Your style.

Wood, polystyrene, or plastic, PollenPal keeps up. Give every hive its own name, color, and photo, name your queens, and see the whole yard at a glance, whether you keep two colonies in the backyard or a few yards across town.

History and reports

Your future self will thank you.

Every inspection is saved to the hive's timeline with your photos. Scroll back through the season and understand what actually happened, instead of guessing. When the season wraps, print an annual report of your whole year.

Do I need the PollenPal device?

No. The app works entirely on its own. The device is a separate product; this is just the inspection software, and it runs on the phone in your pocket.

What do I need to get started?

A phone and your hives. Sign up, add your hives, and log your first inspection in a couple of minutes.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel whenever you like. Your access runs through what you already paid for, and you are not charged again.
